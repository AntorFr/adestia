########## outcome (recorded by hand — the probe was killed, not finished) ##########
Without ca-certificates: `codex --version` and `codex login status` work, but a turn hung
for 13 minutes with no output and no error — the session file frozen at its first write,
the binary still resident — until the container was killed.
With ca-certificates (same image, same command): the turn completed in ~35 s with the
expected 401 retry storm and `turn.failed`.
